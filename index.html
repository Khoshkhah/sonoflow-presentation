Physical Intuition · Why Traffic Varies

The 4 Real-World Drivers of Traffic Volume

Before equations, every vehicle count on any street is shaped by four physical drivers—each with its own distinct uncertainty:

DRIVER 1 · THE STREET

Street Capacity & Baseline

How busy is this street in general?

• Arterials carry 2,000 veh/h, quiet alleys carry 20.
• Uncertainty: We don't have sensors everywhere.
→ Modeled as: Level L(e)
DRIVER 2 · TIME OF DAY

24-Hour Weekly Routine

When do people actually drive here?

• Morning 8 AM rush hour vs. 3 AM quiet night.
• Uncertainty: Different streets peak at different times.
→ Modeled as: Rhythm ρ(e,w,h)
DRIVER 3 · THE CALENDAR

Seasonality & Holidays

What time of year is it?

• Christmas drops, July summer vacation dip.
• Uncertainty: 0% (known from calendar in advance).
→ Modeled as: Calendar k(d)
DRIVER 4 · TODAY'S SHOCK

Weather & Special Events

How did the whole city run today?

• Heavy rain, snow, concerts, protests.
• Uncertainty: Unknown until live sensors report.
→ Modeled as: Day Factor f(d)
+ Natural Daily Randomness (σ_idio): Even when all 4 drivers are known, individual drivers depart minutes apart — creating unavoidable physical scatter.
Why We Need A Model
Core Mathematical Model

Transparent 4-Layer Flow Decomposition

Every hour on every street is broken down into four explainable physical components:

Expected Mean (μ):  log Flow(e,d,h) = L(e) + ρ(e,w,h) + k(d) + λ(e)·f(d)
80% Honest Interval:  [lo, hi] = exp(μ ∓ 1.2816 · σ)
σ² = Var(L(e)) + σ_ρ²(e,w,h) + λ(e)²·Var(f(d)) + σ_idio²(e,w,h)
e: street / edge d: date h: hour (0–23) w: day group (Mon–Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun)
LAYER 1

Baseline / Level (L)

Log-mean flow. How busy is this street?

Uncertainty: Var(L(e)) from Σ
LAYER 2

Rhythm / Shape (ρ)

Log diurnal shape. When is rush hour?

Uncertainty: σ_ρ²(e,w,h)
LAYER 3

Calendar (k)

Holidays & seasonal shifts. Known dates.

Deterministic (Var = 0)
LAYER 4

Day Factor (f)

Weather & special events. Today's shock.

Uncertainty: λ(e)²·Var(f(d))
σ_idio²(e,w,h) (Natural Randomness): Unavoidable day-to-day traffic variation — natural differences in vehicle arrivals that no model or extra sensors can remove.

Operational Engine · Dual Clocks

What We Save and Update Every Day

We maintain a joint Gaussian state in memory: vector L, covariance Σ (2,128 × 2,128), and shape precisions. The operational engine executes five core mathematical operations:

Prerequisite · Input Ingestion
Isolate True Traffic Shift from Sensor Counts
Strips away calendar seasonality $k(d)$ and city-wide weather shocks $\lambda \cdot f(d)$ from incoming counts to extract the true shift.
y* = Count − k(d) − λ·f(d)  |  Shift = y* − L_prior
OP 01 · AGING

Evidence Fading

Past tube counts fade gracefully via daily drift:

Σ += Q_day · Δt  |  P *= α

Unmeasured streets widen confidence bands over unobserved time.

OP 02 · DIFFUSION

Kalman Diffusion

Spreads shift across joint covariance Σ:

L += Σ_{:, s} · S⁻¹ · Shift  |  Σ −= Gain

Updates mean volume (L) and tightens variance across all streets.

OP 03 · SENSORS

24h Shape Learning

Fuses hourly counts into rush-hour curves:

ρ = Fuse(ρ_prior, robs)  |  P_ρ += w

Learns diurnal curve mean (ρ) & shrinks shape error on measured streets.

OP 04 · LEVEL (L)

Level Conservation

Balances baseline flow at junctions:

min (L−L_p)ᵀ Σ⁻¹ (L−L_p)  |  In ≈ Out

Projects baseline volumes (L) onto physical Inflow ≈ Outflow across 3,299 junctions.

OP 05 · SHAPE (ρ)

Shape Propagation

Optimizes network rhythm smoothness:

min ∑ W_ij ‖ρ_i − ρ_j‖²  |  s.t. ρ_M

Harmonizes rush-hour curves (ρ) and shape error (sd_shape) onto unmeasured streets.

Capital Optimization · Sensor Siting

Where to Put the Next Sensor: Uncertain Throughput

How our Movement-Component GMRF scores candidate physical sites by flow-weighted uncertain throughput reduction (veh/h):

THE METRIC

Uncertain Throughput: U = ∑ Flow(e) · σ(e)

Totals the actual uncertain vehicles/hour across all streets in the city:

U = ∑ exp(L(e)) · √Var(L(e))  |  ΔU(s) = ΔU_own + ΔU_reach
  • Why flow-weight? Multiplying baseline flow exp(L(e)) by uncertainty σ(e) = √Var(L(e)) converts statistical variance into physical vehicles/hour, prioritizing busy arterial corridors over quiet alleys.
  • Reach Dominance: For top recommended sites in Södermalm, ΔU_reach accounts for a median 77% of total uncertainty reduction via covariance Σ!
